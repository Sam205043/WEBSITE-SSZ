Hum kya jaankari lete hain

Online admission form bharte samay hum aapse naam, pita/mata ka naam, janm tithi, gender, mobile, WhatsApp number, email, pata, qualification, chuna gaya course aur batch lete hain. Iske saath aap apni photo aur documents (jaise Aadhaar ya marksheet) upload karte hain.

Contact form bharne par sirf naam, mobile, email (agar diya ho) aur aapka message liya jaata hai.

Student login banne ke baad aapki attendance, fee record, assignment submissions aur certificate ki jaankari aapke account se judi rehti hai.

Is jaankari ka istemaal kis liye hota hai

Aapki admission application process karne, Student ID banane, batch allot karne, fees ka record rakhne, attendance aur result maintain karne, aur certificate issue karne ke liye.

Aapko class schedule, fee reminder aur institute ki zaroori soochna bhejne ke liye — SMS, WhatsApp, email ya dashboard notification ke through.

Hum aapka data kisi bhi third party ko bechte nahi hain aur na hi marketing ke liye kisi ko dete hain.

Data kahan store hota hai

Website ka data Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore aur Firebase Storage) par store hota hai, jiske servers India (Mumbai region) me hain. Access sirf institute ke authorised admin ke paas hota hai, aur wo bhi password se surakshit login ke through.

Aapka password hum kabhi dekh nahi sakte — wo Firebase Authentication me encrypted form me rehta hai.

Aapke adhikar

Aap apna data dekhne, usme sudhaar karne ya uska record hataane ke liye institute se kabhi bhi keh sakte hain. Iske liye office aakar ya contact page se sampark karein.

Kanooni ya academic record (jaise issued certificate) se juda data hum record-keeping ke liye rakh sakte hain, chahe aap account band kar dein.

Cookies aur local storage

Website aapke browser me sirf itni jaankari rakhti hai jitni zaroori hai — jaise aapka dark/light theme choice, adhoora bhara hua admission form draft, aur login session. Hum koi advertising ya tracking cookie use nahi karte.

Bachchon ki privacy

18 saal se kam umar ke students ka admission maata-pita ya abhibhavak ki jaankari me hi liya jaata hai. Unka data bhi isi policy ke tahat surakshit rehta hai.

Sampark

Privacy se judi kisi bhi baat ke liye institute office me sampark karein ya contact page se enquiry bhejein.
