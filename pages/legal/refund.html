Fees, installment aur refund se jude niyam — pehle se saaf.

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Koi alag admission fee nahi

Institute koi alag admission ya registration charge nahi leta. Jo course fee bataayi jaati hai, bas wahi deni hoti hai.

Course fee non-refundable hai

Course fee non-refundable hai. Ek baar jama karne ke baad amount wapas nahi hota — chahe admission cancel kiya jaaye ya course beech me chhoda jaaye.

Iski wajah saaf hai: admission hote hi aapke liye seat block ho jaati hai, study material aur book allot ho jaati hai, aur faculty ka time reserve ho jaata hai. Ye sab kharch wapas nahi aata.

Isliye admission lene se pehle course, timing aur fees achhe se samajh lein — poochhne me sankoch na karein. Chahen to ek demo class lekar bhi faisla kar sakte hain.

Installment

Installment par fees li gayi ho to bhi jama kiya gaya amount wapas nahi hota.

Course beech me chhodne par bachi hui installment maaf nahi hoti agar aap certificate ya transfer certificate chahte hain.

Batch transfer — refund ki jagah

Paisa wapas nahi hota, lekin aapka paisa doobta bhi nahi. Ek baar bina kisi extra charge ke aap doosre batch ya doosre course me transfer le sakte hain — naya course mehanga ho to sirf antar dena hoga.

Ye option admission ke 30 din ke andar available hai. Timing me dikkat ho ya kuch aur wajah ho, to pehle humse baat karein — koi na koi raasta nikal aata hai.

Institute dwara cancellation

Yeh niyam sirf student ki taraf se cancel karne par laagu hai. Agar kisi wajah se institute khud batch cancel karta hai aur koi vikalp batch nahi de paata, to aapki poori jama fees wapas ki jaayegi — 10 kaam ke dinon ke andar, usi tareeke se jisse payment hui thi.
