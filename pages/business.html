FAQ

Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Kya aap website bhi banate hain?+

Haan. Business website, landing page aur admin panel wali web app — sab. Website ke saath automation bhi jod sakte hain.

Mujhe computer nahi aata, kya main chala paunga?+

Haan. Sab kuch phone se chalta hai — ek button dabana ya ek line likhna. Staff ko training hum dete hain.

Customer ko message kahan jayega?+

Customer ke WhatsApp par, aapke business ke number se. WhatsApp ka chhota sa kharcha alag hota hai, jo hum pehle hi bata dete hain.

Mera data safe rahega?+

Data aapke apne Google account mein rehta hai. Hum password nahi lete, sirf zaroori access. Clinic ke message mein bimari ka naam kabhi nahi jaata.

Setup mein kitna time lagta hai?+

Chhota setup 3 se 7 din mein. Aapke purane register ka data Sheet mein daalna bhi hum kar dete hain.

Rate kitna hai?+
