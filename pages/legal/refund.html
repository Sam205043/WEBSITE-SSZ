Refund Policy

Fees, installment aur refund se jude niyam — pehle se saaf.

Home/Legal/Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Admission fee

Admission fee (registration charge) non-refundable hai. Yeh seat block karne, student ID banane aur admission process ka kharch cover karti hai.

Course fee refund

Agar aap batch shuru hone se pehle admission cancel karte hain, to course fee ka 90% wapas kiya jaata hai (admission fee chhod kar).

Batch shuru hone ke baad pehle 7 din ke andar cancel karne par 50% course fee wapas hoti hai.

7 din ke baad course fee refundable nahi hoti, kyunki seat, study material aur faculty time allot ho chuke hote hain.

Installment

Installment par li gayi fees me refund sirf jama ki gayi rakam par hi laagu hota hai, poore course fee par nahi.

Course beech me chhodne par bachi hui installment maaf nahi hoti agar aap certificate ya transfer certificate chahte hain.

Batch transfer

Refund ke bajaye aap ek baar bina kisi extra charge ke doosre batch ya doosre course me transfer le sakte hain — course fee ka antar dena hoga agar naya course mehanga ho.

Yeh suvidha admission ke 30 din ke andar hi uplabdh hai.

Refund kaise milega

Refund request likhit roop me (ya student dashboard se) deni hoti hai. Approve hone ke baad 10 kaam ke dinon ke andar usi tareeke se wapas ki jaati hai jisse payment hui thi.

Online payment (Razorpay/UPI) ka refund seedhe usi account me jaata hai. Cash payment ka refund institute office se milta hai.

Institute dwara cancellation

Agar kisi wajah se institute khud batch cancel karta hai aur koi vikalp batch nahi de paata, to poori jama fees (admission fee sahit) wapas ki jaayegi.
